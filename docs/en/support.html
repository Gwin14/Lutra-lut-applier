Lutra Support

Contact us

Open an issue at https://github.com/Gwin14/Lutra-lut-applier/issues describing the problem, your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version. We reply within 2 business days.

My footage looks wrong after the look

Looks expect the clip's log profile. Check that the input profile picked for the clip (for example S-Log3, C-Log or HLG) matches how it was recorded.

Importing LUTs

Lutra imports .cube files from the Files app. You can also share videos straight to Lutra from Photos or other apps.

Exports

Exported videos and frames are saved to your photo library. If saving fails, check in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos that Lutra has access.

Subscription

To manage or cancel, open iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, use "Restore purchases" in the app.
