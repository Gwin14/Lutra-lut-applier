Lutra Privacy Policy

Summary

Lutra has no accounts, shows no ads and has no analytics or tracking. Your videos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded.

Photos and videos

With your permission, Lutra reads the videos you pick from your library, the Files app or the share sheet, applies looks and LUTs on your device, and saves the exported videos and frames back to your library. Videos, LUT files and projects never leave your device.

Data stored on your device

Your projects, settings, imported LUTs and export history are stored only on your device, including the data shared with Lutra's widgets. We have no servers that receive it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple. To validate your subscription, Lutra uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous ID generated by the app and your purchase history, with no personal data. RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing the app. Privacy questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Gwin14/Lutra-lut-applier/issues.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date below will be updated. Last updated: November 11, 2026.
